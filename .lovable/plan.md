## Goal

After the WIN screen has been up for 5 seconds, cut to a new close-up scene: the hero (same character design) thanking the player for making his journey easier and wishing them a good conference, alongside a 16-bit rendition of the MESC 2026 logo. Restart happens only from this scene.

## Art to create

1. `src/assets/game/hero-portrait.png` — a large close-up bust of the existing hero, generated by editing the current `character-sheet.png` idle frame so the face, hat, palette and outline stay identical, just scaled up with more facial detail. Transparent background.
2. `src/assets/game/mesc-2026-logo-16bit.png` — a pixel-art (16-bit / SNES-style, limited palette, chunky dithering, hard outlines) reinterpretation of the attached MESC 2026 conference badge: circular green/gold rings, blue mountain, MESC wordmark, "PORTLAND, OR / AUGUST 17–20, 2026", city skyline and winding river. Rendered as reference-derived pixel art, not the uploaded JPG itself.

Both are loaded through the existing `safeLoadSheet` / fallback path so a missing asset degrades to a drawn placeholder rather than crashing.
